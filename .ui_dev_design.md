# EcosystemSim Game UI Design

## Purpose

Let the player watch and shape a living ecosystem without permanent interface panels obscuring the
map, while retaining quick access to simulation controls, tile contents, and guided evolution.

## Hero Flow

The player spends most of the session watching and navigating the hex map, zooming into active
tiles, selecting populations, and making occasional interventions.

## Hero Surface

The map remains the largest surface. A compact top toolbar contains simulation controls and toggles
for secondary panels. Factions, scenario details, history, help, and selected-tile details can all
be dismissed. Zooming in reveals every population on a tile; zooming out returns to a concise
dominant-population summary.

## Known Context

The game already knows the selected tile, living populations, camera zoom, current calendar,
terrain resources, scenario limits, eligible interventions, and lineage ancestry. The UI must
derive targets and costs from that state rather than asking for IDs or coordinates.

## Proposed Workflows

1. Start a run: choose unrestricted Sandbox, Locust Plague, or Drought Recovery.
2. Track challenge state: see time remaining, action points, objective progress, and final result.
3. Intervene in the locust challenge: select a tile and spend points to cull locusts, restore grass,
   or seed Meganeura.
4. Intervene in the drought challenge: select a stressed land tile and spend points to create
   watering holes, restore vegetation, or seed a temporary rain spell.
5. Inspect population history: open a compact analysis panel, choose a lineage, and understand its
   long-term direction without leaving the map.
6. Review ecosystem feedback: see major events, challenge trends, and stressed tiles as they happen.
7. Inspect terrain succession: inspect degradation/recovery pressure and resulting terrain events.
8. Navigate with compact game chrome: control the simulation and open only the panel currently needed.
9. Inspect complete tile contents: zoom in to see each population on a tile, then zoom out cleanly.
10. Guide evolution: nudge a selected population's traits and branch a named subspecies in Sandbox.

## Workflow Requirements

### 1. Start a run

- **Displayed:** mode name, description, challenge duration, action budget, and victory conditions.
- **Actions:** start Sandbox, Locust Plague, or Drought Recovery.
- **Empty state:** startup overlay remains visible and simulation stays paused until a mode is chosen.
- **Error state:** initialization failure is shown on the overlay without dismissing it.
- **Success feedback:** overlay closes, the selected scenario appears in the status panel, and time begins.

### 2. Track challenge state

- **Displayed:** scenario name, year/day progress, remaining action points, three objective states, and
  final victory/defeat message.
- **Actions:** pause/resume through existing controls, restart the current scenario, or return to scenario selection.
- **Empty state:** Sandbox explains that objectives and action limits are disabled.
- **Error state:** unavailable objective data is shown as an explicit unavailable state.
- **Success feedback:** objective values refresh immediately after ticks and interventions; the simulation
  pauses when the challenge resolves.

### 3. Intervene in the locust challenge

- **Displayed:** selected tile, eligible actions, point costs, and why any action is unavailable.
- **Actions:** cull 99% of locusts within two hexes (1 point), fully restore Graze pools within
  four hexes (2 points), or seed five Meganeura on the selected tile (3 points).
- **Empty state:** no tile selection shows guidance to select an affected land tile.
- **Error state:** inline feedback explains invalid terrain, missing targets, completed challenges, or
  insufficient points.
- **Success feedback:** tile values, action points, and objective progress refresh immediately with a
  concise confirmation message.

### 4. Intervene in the drought challenge

- **Displayed:** selected tile water and vegetation levels, current weather, eligible actions, costs,
  and unavailable reasons.
- **Actions:** create watering holes within three hexes (2 points), restore plant food pools within
  three hexes (2 points),
  or seed a 45-day global rain spell (3 points).
- **Empty state:** no tile selection explains that interventions are applied from a selected land tile.
- **Error state:** inline feedback explains invalid terrain, already-saturated resources, active rain,
  completed challenges, or insufficient points.
- **Success feedback:** resources, weather, action points, and objective progress refresh immediately
  with a concise confirmation message.

### 5. Inspect population history

- **Displayed:** selected lineage, sampled population graph, current/start/min/max values, and net change.
- **Actions:** open or close the analysis panel, switch between History and Events, and choose any
  lineage discovered from the live world.
- **Empty state:** before enough samples exist, explain that history is recorded every 30 days.
- **Error state:** missing lineage data shows an explicit unavailable message rather than a blank chart.
- **Success feedback:** graph and summary refresh on every history sample and immediately after a
  scenario reset.

### 6. Review ecosystem feedback

- **Displayed:** recent major events with day/year and severity, latest-event HUD summary, challenge
  objective trend arrows, and warning markers on stressed or diseased tiles.
- **Actions:** open the Events view and inspect a warning tile through the existing tile-selection flow.
- **Empty state:** show "No major ecosystem events yet" when the run has been quiet.
- **Error state:** unavailable telemetry is stated explicitly and does not hide the map or controls.
- **Success feedback:** interventions and major simulation transitions appear immediately; tile and
  challenge warnings clear automatically when conditions recover.

### 7. Inspect terrain succession

- **Displayed:** active degradation/recovery pressure and transition events with tile coordinates.
- **Actions:** select pressured tiles and inspect their live resources and succession state.
- **Empty state:** no succession section is shown when neither pressure is active.
- **Error state:** unavailable succession settings are stated instead of displaying invalid progress.
- **Success feedback:** terrain color, resource pools, pressure, and event history update together.

### 8. Navigate with compact game chrome

- **Displayed:** calendar, weather, pause/speed state, latest event, and compact panel toggles.
- **Actions:** pause/resume, change speed, restart, choose a scenario, and toggle Factions, Scenario,
  History, and Help.
- **Empty state:** unavailable scenario information remains hidden until requested.
- **Error state:** controls that cannot run before scenario selection remain disabled.
- **Success feedback:** panel visibility changes immediately and toolbar state reflects pause/speed.

### 9. Inspect complete tile contents

- **Displayed:** at close zoom, one visual slot per living population with species icon/initial and count;
  at overview zoom, only the dominant population summary.
- **Actions:** zoom through the detail threshold and select the tile for complete textual details.
- **Empty state:** unpopulated tiles show no content icons.
- **Error state:** species without sprite art use a readable initial/count fallback.
- **Success feedback:** detail mode switches immediately when the camera crosses the threshold.

### 10. Guide evolution

- **Displayed:** remaining guidance points, current size/immunity traits, mutation costs, and subspecies
  naming controls for the selected population.
- **Actions:** nudge size larger/smaller, improve immunity, expand/collapse controls, and split a named
  subspecies from the current population.
- **Empty state:** non-Sandbox scenarios explain that guided evolution is unavailable.
- **Error state:** inline feedback covers insufficient points, invalid/duplicate names, trait bounds,
  extinct populations, and populations too small to branch.
- **Success feedback:** points and traits refresh immediately; a successful branch appears on the tile,
  retains its lineage root, and creates a located ecosystem event.
